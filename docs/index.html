Hearth

Your Nest thermostat, in the menu bar.

Hearth is a small Mac app that puts the temperature at the top of your screen. Click it to get a dial and full control: heat, cool, auto, eco and the fan. No phone required.

Download for Mac macOS 15 or later. Free.

How it works

Glance, click, done

  • 01Look upThe menu bar shows the inside temperature. The icon changes when the system is heating, cooling or in Eco.
  • 02Click to open the dialDrag the knob, scroll over the dial, or use the + and - buttons and arrow keys. The change goes to the thermostat once you stop adjusting.
  • 03Pick a modeHeat, Cool, Auto or Off. In Auto you get two knobs, one for heat and one for cool, kept a safe distance apart.
  • 04Leave itHearth refreshes every minute on its own, checks more often right after a change, and pauses while your Mac sleeps.

Features

Everything the thermostat does

Every modeHeat, Cool, Auto and Off, with a separate heat and cool setpoint in Auto.
EcoTurn Eco on when you leave. Hearth shows the range it holds.
Fan timerRun the fan for 15 minutes up to 12 hours, and stop it early.
More than one thermostatSwitch between them from the name at the top of the panel.
Humidity and statusInside temperature, humidity, and whether it is heating, cooling or idle.
Stays out of the wayNo Dock icon. Opens at login if you want. Works with VoiceOver.

What you need

Sign in once

Open Hearth and click Sign in with Google. Your browser opens. Pick your home and thermostat on Google's screen, and you're done. No developer accounts, no fees.

  • MACmacOS 15 or later
  • THERMOSTATA Nest thermostat set up in the Google Home app
  • ACCOUNTThe Google account that owns it

Your data

Between your Mac and Google

Hearth has no server, no accounts and no analytics. It talks to Google and nothing else. Your sign-in tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain, and thermostat readings are only kept in memory while the app runs.
